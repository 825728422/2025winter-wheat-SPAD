This directory is a partial peer-review reproducibility package. It contains a held-out processed test subset, low-resolution RGB examples, model settings, the mask-sensitivity summary, and verification code. It supports checks of the supplied subset and reported processing summaries; it cannot reproduce the complete analysis. The mask-sensitivity table contains 31 threshold alternatives without the booting extent plus one extent-based reconstruction; that extent row is an independent reconstruction rather than an exact replication of the original mask-transfer operation. The full dataset, original high-resolution UAV imagery, and project-specific raw files are not included. Further redistribution or release of restricted material requires author and project permission. The public repository URL is supplied separately once the authors complete the upload.
